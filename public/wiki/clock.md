## What is Clock & Stopwatch Mode?

When you don't need a structured countdown:

- **Stopwatch**: Count up from zero with millisecond accuracy. Ideal for timing laps on an auto-belay route, endurance traverses, or campus board challenges.
- **Dual Splits**: Split buttons allow timing two climbers independently on the same continuous clock.
- **Gym Wall Clock**: Displays real-time 24-hour time synchronized with real-time hardware clock (RTC).

---

## How to Activate & Start

### 1. Select Clock Mode

- **From Phone / Web UI**: Open the menu, select **Clock** under *Climb Mode*.
- **From Hardware Station**: Rotate knob to `CLOCK` and press to select.

### 2. Operate the Stopwatch

- Click **START** in the Web App or press **Button 0 (Action)** on the station.
- The clock begins counting up smoothly from `00:00.0`.
- Press **RESET** anytime to stop and zero the clock.

---

## Stopwatch & Split Times

While the stopwatch is running, you can log split times without interrupting the master timer:

- **WINNER A (Split A)**: Press to freeze and record the time for Lane A or Climber 1.
- **WINNER B (Split B)**: Press to freeze and record the time for Lane B or Climber 2.
- **Continuous Master Clock**: The master clock keeps running so you can capture sequential checkpoint splits during an endurance test or continuous traverse.

---

## Wall Clock & Timezone Sync

The station features an internal battery-backed real-time clock (RTC).

- **Sync with Phone Clock**:
  In the Web App under **⚙️ Configuration ➔ System & Timezone**, click **🕒 Sync System Time with Browser**.
  The timer immediately syncs to your phone's exact local time.

- **Timezone Presets**:
  Choose your local region (e.g. *Europe/Helsinki*, *UTC*, *US Eastern*, *Japan*). The station adjusts automatically.
